Ten working guides prepared from the FMRC unrestricted practice management course, checked against the legislation in force and the Law Society of NSW's published material, and written to be added to over time. The Day 1 material in one place. It begins with the market and the profit driver equation, moves through the practical toolkit and the full fixed fee and cost of production analyses, supplies an office manual index and a managing partner job description, and finishes with business planning, ethics and business development. ### 1.2 Trust Accounting - executive summary

A complete compliance reference for trust money in NSW under the Legal Profession Uniform Law and General Rules. Every obligation carries a hyperlinked citation to the NSW legislation register. Three changes since the course materials were written are flagged: the Application Regulation was remade in 2025, the Financial Transaction Reports Act was repealed, and tranche 2 AML obligations commenced. ### 1.4 Risk Management - executive summary

Four numbers frame the guide: conveyancing produces 37% of claims, poor communication causes 42% of them, not knowing the law causes 23%, and over 90% of cyber claims are business email compromise. Claims are overwhelmingly about contributing factors rather than black letter law. The guide covers the professional indemnity policy, where claims come from, the clients who bite, supervision as a conduct obligation and a claims driver, business management as a risk, and the cyber exposure including the double excess.

### 1.6 Stress Management and Wellbeing - executive summary

The Australian legal profession has roughly four times the depression rate of other professions and two thirds of people with a mental health condition never seek help. This guide takes a harm-minimisation approach: what stress is doing physiologically, how to recognise it in yourself, two in-the-moment techniques that work in forty seconds, the self-assessment activity with scoring, and the ongoing practices that sustain wellbeing. It closes with the employer's statutory duty to manage psychosocial hazards.

## Part 5 - Colophon text

**About these guides**

These guides were prepared from the FMRC unrestricted practice management course - its slide decks, seminar notes and session recordings - synthesised into practical reference form and checked against the legislation as in force on the NSW legislation register, the Law Society of NSW's published guidance including the *Legal Accounting Handbook*, AUSTRAC and Australian Taxation Office guidance, and Lawcover's risk management material.

They are a personal and firm reference, not legal advice, and not a substitute for the primary sources. The law is stated as at the date shown at the head of each guide. Where the course materials had been overtaken by legislative change, the change is identified in the text.

Notes and edits added through the application are the reader's own and are marked as such.
